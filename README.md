## About

**Cadence** is designed to give you precise control over your local music library's metadata. Whether you're cleaning up tags, organizing album art, or structuring your personal collection, Cadence provides a streamlined interface to get your audio files properly sorted and tagged.

---

## Features

- **Metadata Editor:** Easily inspect and modify ID3 / file tags (Artist, Album, Title, Track Number, Genre, Year, etc.).
- **Library Organization:** Keep your local audio files organized and structured cleanly.
- **Artwork Management:** Embed, update, or remove cover art effortlessly.
- **Lightweight & Fast:** Built for performance with a modern, responsive layout.
